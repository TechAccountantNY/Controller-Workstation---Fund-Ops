# Brindlewood Fund Complex — Fee Schedule (extract, effective 1 Jan 2026)

All figures fabricated for assessment use.

## Management fee
1.25% per annum per underlying sleeve, **accrued monthly on the average of the
sleeve's beginning and ending monthly NAV** (annual rate / 12).

## Incentive fee
15% of net new profits per sleeve, **accrued monthly only above the sleeve's
high-water mark**, crystallised annually on 31 December. No accrual while a
sleeve's NAV is at or below its HWM.

## Administration fee
Lanyard Fund Services (Cayman) Ltd: $4,500 per vehicle per month, flat, billed quarterly in arrears.
